TRoyVibe™ — from prototype to production

Teaching agents — and people — to actually understand each other.

TRoyAI's departments already shared knowledge with each other, but what actually got shared was a raw wall of unstructured text: no intent, no confidence marker, no honest flag for uncertainty. TRoyVibe™ now gates every brief that reaches TRoyAI's CEO Assistant before any department is delegated work.
